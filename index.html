Uzhavan Hub – AI Decision Engine Architecture

💡 The All-in-One Digital Farming Ecosystem Uzhavan Hub is an AI-powered all-in-one digital farming platform that helps farmers make better farming decisions and easily access agricultural services, resources, workers, machinery, and livestock healthcare.
1. 🌱 FARM DECISION

AI crop recommendation, agro-weather advisories, soil health, fertilizer scheduling, crop health scanner, and net profit predictions.

2. 🛠️ FARM SERVICES

Farm worker booking (planting, harvesting, weeding) and machinery rental (tractors, combine harvesters, rotavators, sprayers).

3. 🐄 LIVESTOCK CARE

Nearby veterinary hospitals, doctors, cattle health guides (cow, buffalo, goat, sheep, poultry), and 24/7 emergency assistance (1962).

4. 💰 FARM FINANCE

Wholesale mandi spot prices with "Sell or Wait?" trend insights, PM-KISAN & solar pump schemes, and 4% KCC crop credit.

5. 🎙️ FARMER ASSISTANCE

Bilingual "Uzhavan AI" voice assistant supporting spoken Tamil (தமிழ்) and English Speech-to-Text and audio synthesis grounded in the farmer's actual profile.
